# Legal and provenance review

This is an engineering inventory and set of counsel questions, not legal advice.

## Repository code

- Repository license: Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE`).
- New product code is authored in this repository under that license.
- Dependency licenses must be captured in the release SBOM/container scan before distribution.

## Models and tooling

- The referenced [Unsloth Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct artifact](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct/tree/main) displays Apache-2.0.
- The referenced [4-bit artifact](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct-bnb-4bit/tree/main) also displays Apache-2.0.
- [Unsloth's core repository](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth/) is Apache-2.0, while separate Studio/CLI surfaces may have different terms. Confirm exactly which packages and artifacts were used.
- Fine-tuned weights are private on the owner's Modal volume. They are not bundled or published by this release.

Counsel/owner gate: trace the full base-model chain, package versions, training recipe, and redistribution requirements before publishing weights or selling hosted inference.

## Training and evaluation data

- The bundled product sample is deterministic synthetic data generated by this repository.
- Synthetic datasets should preserve generator version, seed, and any font/theme inputs.
- `real_v0` uses eight public OWID charts/CSVs for evaluation. Confirm source terms, attribution, and whether images/data may be redistributed; do not treat evaluation access as a license to bundle customer-facing assets.
- Frontier-provider outputs and model names in saved research receipts remain subject to provider terms and may become stale.

Counsel/owner gate: produce a dataset bill of materials and signed provenance statement for every artifact used to train distributed weights.

## Fonts and UI assets

- Bundled IBM Plex Sans WOFF2 files are from Fontsource's IBM Plex Sans package.
- The full SIL Open Font License is included at `unrender/product/static/fonts/LICENSE.txt`.
- No customer or third-party chart is bundled in the product UI.

## Product name and claims

- Run a trademark/domain review for “Unrender” before paid launch.
- Do not claim exact extraction, frontier-model superiority, contamination-free real-world performance, compliance certification, uptime, or production readiness without corresponding evidence.
- Use “extracted” until a person explicitly approves the result; approval records review, not factual truth.

## Privacy and commercial terms

The current privacy and terms pages are conspicuous drafts. Required owner inputs include legal entity, physical/contact address, governing law, processor/subprocessor list, deployment region, retention/deletion policy, security commitments, refund terms, support channel, age restriction, and incident-notification obligations.

No customer data or live payment should be accepted until counsel approves these documents and the operator can fulfill their promises.
