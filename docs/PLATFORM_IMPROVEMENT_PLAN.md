Date: 2026-09-23. Baseline: main `584db2d`. Scope: the 30 findings in the September 23 platform audit. The owner requested implementation, tests, Simplify, final Code Review, push and deployment.

Changes will be committed as coherent stages rather than one unreviewable patch. Existing user work in the Desktop checkout remains untouched.

## Decisions and external dependencies

- Requested from owner: daily inference budget and business identity/support details. Until supplied, do not invent legal identity, prices, provider costs or claim a dollar cap.
- Billing/email activation requires valid provider setup and owner-reviewed terms. Engineering can improve readiness and the honest beta path without silently enabling payment or sending email.
- Independent model annotations, external accessibility/security certification, and customer studies cannot be generated by changing code. Build repeatable checks and retain explicit acceptance gates.
- Do not change model weights or provider runtime merely to claim lower latency. Preserve the pinned release until a quality-controlled candidate passes.
- Keep SQLite on its single owner host; optimize measured hot paths before considering a storage architecture migration.

## Validation and release policy

Use isolated data for saturation, accounting, deletion and recovery tests. Production validation is bounded and non-destructive. Run relevant tests after each stage, then the full suite, lint/types and browser acceptance after integration. Preserve exact-version approval, tenant isolation, durable credit accounting and ambiguous-dispatch handling. Deployment is authorized by the user; access or missing owner facts will be reported specifically if they block an action.
