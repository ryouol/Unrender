The service

Unrender helps a user convert a chart image or selected PDF page into an editable table, review it against the source, record corrections and approval, and export CSV, JSON, or XLSX. Model output can be wrong. The user is responsible for reviewing values before relying on or distributing them.

Permitted use

Users must have the right to upload and process each source. Users may not submit unlawful content, personal data they are not authorized to process, malware, or files intended to overload or probe the service. Usage limits, file limits, and rate limits may be enforced to protect the service and control GPU cost.

Credits

One chart extraction or reprocessing attempt uses one credit. A reserved credit is returned automatically only when a job fails or is cancelled before the inference provider is dispatched. Once provider dispatch is recorded, the attempt consumes the credit even if the provider later fails or cancellation arrives; this prevents repeated paid inference at no cost. The saved replay sample may be offered without variable inference cost. Paid credit-pack pricing, expiry, and any discretionary support remedy must be finalized by the operator and counsel before launch.

No accuracy warranty

Outputs are estimates from visual source material. Unrender does not warrant exact extraction, survey-grade measurement, fitness for a regulated decision, or superiority to another model. Users should retain and cite the original source.
